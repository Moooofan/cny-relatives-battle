# 結算稱號門檻調校報告

Generated by `src/engine/sim.ts` (`simulateRun` / `simulateMany` / `aggregate`), driven from
`src/engine/__tests__/balance.test.ts`'s sibling analysis run (not committed — a throwaway
`_tmp-analysis.test.ts` was used to produce the numbers below, then deleted; the smoke test that
stays in the repo is `balance.test.ts`). Methodology: 300 seeds × 4 policies × 4 modes = 4800 full
runs through the real reducer (`createGame → applyOption/applySpecial → advance`) against the real
`CONTENT` bundle (8 bosses, ~680 questions currently in the pool, 30 lives — 1000-question fill
from other agents is in progress, see caveats).

game state sequence regardless of which policy is scripted against it.

## 5. Sanity findings

**Boss win rate outliers (casual policy, random mode, N≈35-55 fights per boss):**

| boss | tier | win rate | verdict |
|---|---|---|---|
| ama (阿嬤) | hard (special) | 7.4% | **< 20%** — flagged |
| sangu (三姑, final) | final | 11.8% | **< 20%** — flagged |
| sanjiuma | hard | 38.9% | ok |
| guzhang | hard | 40.0% | ok |
| dabo | normal | 60.0% | ok |
| biaojie | normal | 69.7% | ok |
| xiao-biaodi | easy | 73.0% | ok |
| neighbor-chen | easy | 84.6% | ok |

No boss exceeded 95%. `ama` and `sangu` are the two bosses an average ("casual") player loses to
most of the time in a single random encounter — worth a second look from whoever owns
`archetypes.ts`/boss modifiers, though this report only measures, it does not touch mechanics.

**三姑 (final) beatable by `good` in gauntlet ≥ 25%?** Yes — 63.2% win rate (n=220 fights reached).
Comfortably clears the bar; gauntlet's per-win heal (+20 HP) and rest-stop (+40 HP every 3 wins)
make the final boss noticeably more beatable there than as a cold-open in random mode.

**Does story mode's ama fight ever produce "阿嬤的乖孫" (grandma-favorite)?** **No — 0 out of 1200
simulated story runs** (300 seeds × all 4 policies) produced it; the split was 768 `lost` / 432
`harmony`, nothing else. Root cause: `computeStoryEnding` returns `"lost"` immediately on any
player death regardless of how the ama fight went, and `casual` never survives the full story
(0% win rate) — it always resolves to `lost` before the ama-specific check runs. `good`/`expert`
do survive, but the ending also requires **zero `perfect` (and zero `landmine`) picks across the
entire ama fight**, and both policies pick `perfect` too often (55%/85% per turn) to go an ~10-turn
fight without one by chance.

This is not a dead branch, though: a one-off hand-scripted policy (deflect/meek/backfire-only
against ama, perfect/deflect elsewhere, otherwise identical rules) was run for 40 seeds and hit
`grandma-favorite` **40/40 times**. So the ending is easy to reach *if you specifically know to go
soft on ama* — none of the 4 spec'd policies model that boss-specific behavior, which is why it
never shows up in the main sweep. Worth flagging to content/UX: either document the "be gentle with
ama" trick somewhere discoverable, or it stays a secret nobody finds by playing normally. The same
structural issue (an overall `lost` short-circuits the check) likely makes `"apprentice"` (needs
`landmineCount >= 5` *and not* losing) similarly hard to reach under normal play, though that one
was not separately re-verified with a scripted policy.

intended pacing. In other words the final-boss *length* is currently a function of player skill
more than of tuned HP/power, which is worth a mechanics look (out of this report's scope).

## 6. Assumptions

- Content pool is ~680/1000 questions at simulation time (other agents are still writing
  `src/content/questions/**`); thresholds were tuned against whatever `CONTENT` resolves to right
  now. Re-run `simulateMany` once the pool hits 1000 questions — percentiles may shift slightly as
  more topic variety smooths out per-boss variance, though the overall shape (dominated by the
  ±0/±100 boss-defeat lump sum and the crit-combo fat tail) should hold.
- "Random policy almost never exceeds r3" (spec item 3) is treated as "rare, not literally never":
  after tuning, blind-random still reaches r4+ ≈ 16-18% of the time in random/daily (vs ≈ 8-9% in
  story/gauntlet). Pushing the r3/r4 boundary higher to suppress this further would pull `casual`'s
  r1-r3 share well below its 50% target, so accuracy to the `casual` spec was prioritized; this
  trade-off is a direct result of the crit/combo mechanic giving even blind play an occasional
  large score.
- `lifeId` was left unset in every simulated run, so `createGame` picks a random life per seed (as
  the task specified) — thresholds are therefore already averaged across all 30 lives' modifiers.
- Rounding: every threshold is a multiple of 10 (300/350/400/440/725/etc. are also multiples of 25).
